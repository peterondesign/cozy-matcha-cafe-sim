# Video-to-game spec

Sources: the supplied gameplay clip, the supplied illustrated reference screenshot, sampled video frames, and the generated clean cafe plate.

- **Camera:** fixed, straight-on first-person barista POV from behind the counter. Customer centered behind the work surface; garden and timber cafe fill the background.
- **Hero:** the player is the unseen barista/camera. Customers wait across the counter with unique idle animation and a picture bubble containing their order.
- **Mechanic:** symbolic action HUD only. The hot and iced recipes alternate and use distinct steps. Whisking uses a rhythm gauge; the precision finish requires stopping the line inside the small green zone and releasing a circular hold arc precisely at its target.
- **Feedback:** persistent Order Quality in the HUD; animated guide paths and HUD pulses for quality changes; animated coin collection and a three-star tip; readable service status and result card.
- **World:** cozy garden cafe with counter foreground, grouped ingredient illustrations, timber framing, blossoms, and warm morning light.
- **Look:** hand-painted storybook illustration, warm wood, muted olive and sage, cream ceramics, pink blossoms, soft shadows, leaf movement, drifting petals, and fine paper texture.
- **Players:** one.
